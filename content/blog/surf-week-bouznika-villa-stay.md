A **surf week Bouznika Morocco villa stay** is not a cruise itinerary. It is six nights with the Atlantic in your face, a kitchen, hot water after salt, and someone who knows whether you should paddle at 7:00 or go back to sleep.

We host that week for 8,500 dh. The weekend version is 1,500 dh if you only have a slice of time. This article is about the week, because that is when Morocco actually teaches you.

## Why a week beats three heroic day trips

Driving from Casablanca three times sounds cheaper until you count petrol, traffic, and the sessions you miss because you arrived to onshore junk.

When you [stay in the oceanfront villa](/surf-guiding) you surf **windows**, not appointments. Dawn if it is clean. A second look in the afternoon. A rest when the wind ruins it. That is how you get ten water hours instead of three exhausted ones.

The house is set up for that: beach access, showers, WiFi if you still have to send one email, space for a board that is drying on the terrace.

## A realistic rhythm

**Day 1.** Arrive, unpack, look at the water. Light session or a [first lesson](/surf-coach) if you have never stood up. Early night.

**Days 2–4.** The work. Guided sessions on the best-looking banks. Video on your phone is optional; listening in the water is not. Eat properly. Stretch. If you ding a board, we glass it instead of pretending tape is a personality.

**Day 5.** Often a rest morning or a shorter free surf. Fatigue is when people jump and land wrong.

**Days 6–7.** Use whatever swell you are given. Sometimes it is the best of the week. Sometimes it is small and technical. Either way you are here, not on the highway.

We do not promise barrels. We promise that you will not spend the week guessing.

## Who this week is for

- Beginners who want the pop-up to **stick**
- Intermediates from Casa/Rabat who never get consecutive days
- Small groups of friends who will actually share a kitchen
- Someone travelling with a non-surfer who still wants a terrace and a beach

It is not for people who need a party hostel and a different bar every night. Bouznika is calmer than that. If that sentence bothers you, book the south.

## What to bring (and what the house already is)

Bring a swimsuit, sunscreen, a charger, and honesty about your level. Boards and wetsuits can come from us. If you have a quiver, bring it — and budget time for [dings](/board-repair), because a week of waves finds every weak glass job.

Leave the fantasy of surfing six hours a day. Two quality sessions and a nap is a good Atlantic day.

Custom packages exist when your dates or group size do not match the card on the site. [Contact](/contact) is the door.

A surf week here is simple on purpose: sleep near the peak, paddle when it is worth it, eat, repair what breaks, go home with salt in your ears and a pop-up you do not have to think about. That is the stay.
