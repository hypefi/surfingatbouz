**Foam blank surfboard shaping basics Morocco** is a search full of workshop porn: planers, dust, a silhouette against the sun. That craft is real. It is also not what most people landing in Casablanca need this week.

I repair and restore boards in Bouznika. I will explain the blank honestly, tell you when a custom makes sense, and tell you when you should [glass the ding](/board-repair) and go surfing.

## What a blank is

A **blank** is the foam core before it is a surfboard: roughly board-shaped, waiting for a planer and a template. Traditional PU blanks take polyester resin and fibreglass. Many modern lights are EPS meant for epoxy.

From there the shaper cuts:

- **Outline** — how wide, how the nose and tail sit in the water  
- **Rocker** — the curve along the length; too much and a beginner board is a pig to paddle  
- **Foil and rails** — thickness distribution; this is feel  
- **Stringer** (on many PUs) — the wooden spine  

Then **glassing**: cloth, resin, fins or boxes, sanding, gloss if you want it. Paint can live in that stack — see [custom painting in Bouznika](/blog/custom-surfboard-painting-bouznika).

None of this is a rainy-afternoon craft project. Dust in lungs, resin on time, mistakes you cannot unsaw.

## Morocco’s relationship with the craft

This country has shapers, glassers, and a huge culture of **keeping boards alive** because importing a quiver is expensive and slow. The unsung majority of the work is not a brand-new shortboard for a visiting influencer. It is:

- Closing Atlantic dings  
- Replacing a cooked fin box  
- Fairing a yellowed PU so it is a board again  
- Teaching kids on foamies that get destroyed and patched

That is the ecosystem [our shop](/board-repair) sits in. Shaping knowledge makes repairs better. Repair volume pays for the patience a real custom needs.

## When a custom from a blank is the right call

- You have **ridden enough** to know you want a 9'1" with a certain nose, not “a surfboard, any surfboard”  
- You can **wait** — blanks, glass, and cure do not care about your return flight  
- You will **be here** for a fitting conversation, not a WhatsApp silhouette  
- You understand a first custom might still not be “the one,” because feel is learned

When it is the wrong call: you have never stood up, you want it for Saturday, you want it cheaper than a supermarket soft-top. Learn first. [Lessons](/surf-coach) are how you discover whether you even like a longboard.

## Repair and restore as “almost shaping”

A bad delam job is sculpture: cut until you find dry foam, rebuild, cloth, fair. You are reshaping a wound. Travellers call it repair. In the hands it is the same attention as a blank, just starting from a scar.

If your uncle’s 90s PU still has rocker, restoring it can be more soul and less waste than a new pop-out. If the foam is mud, we will say no. Sentiment does not float.

## Materials on this coast

UV is not gentle. Dark tints, neglected dings, and roof racks without a bag cook decks. [PU vs epoxy](/blog/pu-vs-epoxy-ding-repair-morocco) is required reading before you pick a construction for a board that will live in a Moroccan summer.

Fins: boxes fail. 500 dh is on the menu because a board without a fin is a sled. Do not wait until the box is a hole in the ocean.

## If you are curious, start as a guest of the craft

Ask to see a repair in progress. Watch how cloth wets out. That will teach you more than a motivational video of a planer. If you later want a blank discussed, [contact](/contact) with what you ride now, your weight, and whether the board will live in Bouznika or fly home in a bag.

Foam is humble. The ocean is not. Shape with respect or repair with respect — both are the same job of putting a human on a wave without the board coming apart. In Bouznika we take that job seriously, whether the starting point is a blank or a crack.
