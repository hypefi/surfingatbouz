**Custom surfboard painting Bouznika** sits in a strange place between craft and Instagram. People want a board that looks like it belongs on this coast — colour, a name, a pattern — and they also want to paddle tomorrow.

In the shop, paint is the last chapter. Glass is the book. If you skip that order, the Atlantic peels your artwork off a wet rail and you will think paint “does not last in Morocco.” Paint lasts. Water under paint does not.

## Repair first, always

Walk in with photos of the **whole board**, not only the tail you want gold. I look for:

- Open dings and wet foam
- Soft decks
- Fin boxes that have already started to walk
- PU yellowing that will show through a light colour

[Ding repair](/blog/surfboard-ding-repair-bouznika) is the unromantic half. [PU vs epoxy](/blog/pu-vs-epoxy-ding-repair-morocco) decides which pigments and resins we even pick up.

If the board is structurally tired, we talk restoration: fair the foam, new cloth, then colour. That is shaping-adjacent work, not a Sharpie.

## What looks good after a season of sand

Bouznika is a beach break. Boards get dragged, stacked, sat on.

- **Bold blocks and strong lines** age with dignity
- **Tiny script** becomes a ghost
- **Metallic everything** looks like a rental after UV
- **Dark decks** show wax and knees; light decks show everything else — pick your poison

A Moroccan motif, a simple wave, a child’s drawing transferred large — those can be beautiful. A photoreal portrait on a rail you will smash in shorebreak is a heartbreak we can warn you about in advance.

## The process, without mystique

1. Agree the design and the **deadline**  
2. Fix structure  
3. Sand to a surface colour can hold  
4. Colour (sometimes under a lamination, sometimes in the hot coat — depends on the board)  
5. Cure, sand, finish  
6. You wax it and try not to throw it nose-first for one week

Resin has a smell and a clock. Winter cold slows us. Summer dust and flies have opinions too. This is a workshop next to an ocean, not a climate-controlled auto booth.

## Prices and expectations

The public menu on [board repair](/board-repair) covers dings, boxes, plugs, delam. Paint and full restore are **quoted**, because a pinstripe is not a mural, and a mural on a rotten PU is not a mural.

Bring the board. If you are still in Casa, WhatsApp photos. If you want a full custom shape from a blank, that is a longer conversation than painting an existing board — see the [foam blank basics](/blog/foam-blank-surfboard-shaping-basics-morocco) if you are curious about that craft. Painting what you already ride is the usual request, and it is enough of a craft on its own.

## How to brief a painter (me)

- One or two reference images, not a Pinterest folder of forty  
- Colours you can name  
- Whether the board is a **daily driver** or a wall piece (be honest)  
- Flight date if you have one  

Then let the resin do what resin does. A rushed gloss for a plane is how we both dislike each other.

Colour is joy. Structure is why you still have a board to colour. In Bouznika we do both, in that order. Message [contact](/contact) when you are ready to show the patient.
